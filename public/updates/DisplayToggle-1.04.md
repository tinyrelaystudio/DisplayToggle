# Display Toggle 1.04

## New

- New Rules section in the menu. The first rule, "Built-in off with external,"
  is on by default: while an external display is connected, the built-in
  display is turned off and its switch is locked. Unplugging the external
  display turns the built-in display back on.
- Checks for updates when the app starts and every hour. When an update is
  available, the menu shows "Update Available" at the top of the update item.

Requires macOS 13 Ventura or later. A purchase includes updates through Display
Toggle 2.x.
