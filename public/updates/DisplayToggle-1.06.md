# Display Toggle 1.06

## Fix

- When the MacBook lid is closed and an external display is connected, the
  built-in display now turns off right away, even while the menu is open.
- While that applies, the built-in display switch in the menu is locked and
  cannot turn the built-in display back on.

Requires macOS 13 Ventura or later. A purchase includes updates through Display
Toggle 2.x.
